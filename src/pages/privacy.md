# Privacy

News Shuffle stores your shuffle filters and theme in this browser. You can remove them by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Your browser requests source icons from Google, using each source's hostname. Google receives the request and its connection details. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We use Google Analytics to measure page visits and use of the Open button. Google can receive cookieless measurements while consent is denied. Shuffle events contain neither your filters nor the source you opened.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies to serve ads based on previous visits to this or other sites, subject to your privacy choices. If you see a consent message, use Privacy and cookie settings at the bottom of each page to change or withdraw your choice. Learn [how Google uses data from partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) or adjust personalised ads in [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

When you open a source, that website handles your visit under its own privacy practices.

For questions, email [privacy@mickschroeder.com](mailto:privacy@mickschroeder.com).
