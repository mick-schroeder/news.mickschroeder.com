News Shuffle takes you to a random news source.

Most of us read the same few sites. News Shuffle is a way out of that habit:
pick the topics, regions and feeds you care about, shuffle, and land on a
newsroom you might never have found.

The list is rebuilt every night from the sites that news feeds like Newzit,
Techmeme and the Drudge Report surface most. An AI editor reviews each new
source, and a source no feed has listed for three months is retired. Sources
are ranked by reach and influence, never by viewpoint.

News Shuffle is made by Mick Schroeder. The
[source list](https://github.com/mick-schroeder/popular-news-sites), its
[data](https://data.news.mickschroeder.com/sources.json) and the
[site](https://github.com/mick-schroeder/news.mickschroeder.com) are open
source.
